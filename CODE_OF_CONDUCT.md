# Code of Conduct

This Code of Conduct applies to all repositories, issues, pull requests and discussions of the FRAME-ITS organisation.

## Purpose

FRAME is maintained openly by organisations and people from across Europe. We expect everybody to keep the collaboration respectful, constructive and professional.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and considerate
- Give constructive, specific feedback
- Use inclusive language
- Focus on technical accuracy and clarity
- Collaborate in good faith

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment or discrimination
- Personal attacks
- Disruptive or aggressive communication
- Knowingly providing false or misleading information

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behaviour, write to contact@frame-online.eu. Do not use a public issue for this. Reports are handled discreetly by the FRAME Maintainers.

## Enforcement

The FRAME Maintainers may take appropriate action, including warnings, rejection of issues or pull requests, and temporary or permanent exclusion from contributing.
